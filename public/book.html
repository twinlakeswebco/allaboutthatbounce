Good to know.

When is my date booked?

Only when we confirm your request. Sending the form does not hold a date, and you will not be charged anything online.

Where do you deliver?

Oldham, Shelby, Henry, Trimble and Jefferson counties in Kentucky. If your event is somewhere else, call 502-851-7596 and we’ll talk it through.

How long is the rental?

Eight hours are guaranteed. Delivery and pickup times are confirmed personally. Extra hours or overnight use may be available by arrangement, depending on the next booking.

Do you deliver and set up?

Yes. We deliver, set up, take down, and pick up. Setup takes about 30–45 minutes, and we walk through the safety rules with you before the fun starts.

When will I get my delivery time?

We’ll contact you during the week of your event and text your delivery ETA the evening before, sometimes as late as 10 p.m. while we finalize our route. The start and end times on your request tell us when your party begins and ends; they are not delivery times. You’re welcome to call us with any questions.

How do I pay?

The remaining balance is due at delivery, before setup. We take cash or check. Exact cash is appreciated. Checks carry a $10 service fee; returned checks have a $35 fee. Please have payment ready when we arrive. Someone must be present to sign the waiver. We cannot set up without payment or accept payment at pickup.

Do I need a deposit?

A $100 deposit applies once per booking for rentals totaling $375 or more, credited toward your balance. We provide payment instructions after approving your request. No payment is collected on this form.

How far ahead should I book?

Peak season runs April through October. Book several weeks ahead for a bounce house, and up to about three months ahead for big events like school or church carnivals. Last-minute plans? We’re happy to help when we can.

Is there a delivery fee?

A delivery fee applies beyond 15 miles. Your estimate shows it when you check your address, and we confirm it with you.

What if the weather turns?

We do not set up outdoors below 50°F, during thunderstorms, or with winds over 15 mph, including gusts. Rain alone does not necessarily mean cancellation; we will discuss conditions with you. If we cancel for any reason, including weather, your deposit is refunded. If your plans change, please give us at least 48 hours’ notice when possible. Contact us to discuss cancellations and any deposit arrangements.

Can I have a bounce house at a park?

Absolutely. Reserve the space and get any permits you need. Ask the park if electricity is available. If not, you’ll need to bring a generator.

Planning a commercial or public event?

Pricing and policies may differ. Please call 502-851-7596 to talk it through.
