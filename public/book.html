Good to know.

How long is the rental?

All day, based on an 8-hour day. We deliver in the morning, usually between 7 and 11 a.m., and pickup starts at 6 p.m. We’ll call the day before to confirm times. Need something different? Just ask.

Do you deliver and set up?

Yes. We deliver, set up, take down, and pick up. Setup takes about 30–45 minutes, and we walk through the safety rules with you before the fun starts.

How do I pay?

Payment is due in full at delivery, before setup. We take cash or check. Exact cash is appreciated, and returned checks have a $35 fee.

Do I need a deposit?

No deposit is needed to hold your date. Plans change, so please let us know as soon as you can if yours do.

How far ahead should I book?

Peak season runs April through October. Book several weeks ahead for a bounce house, and up to about three months ahead for big events like school or church carnivals. Last-minute plans? We’re happy to help when we can.

Is there a delivery fee?

A delivery fee applies beyond 15 miles. Your estimate shows it when you enter your address, and the owner confirms it with you.

What if the weather turns?

Cancel for rain or wind with no fee, up to 2 hours before your drop-off time. We can’t set up in high winds or active rain, and safety always comes first.

Can I have a bounce house at a park?

Absolutely. Reserve the space and get any permits you need. Ask the park if electricity is available. If not, you’ll need to bring a generator.

Planning a commercial or public event?

Pricing and policies may differ. Please call 502-851-7596 to talk it through.
