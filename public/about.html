A local family business that brings the party to your yard.

Meet Rena & the business.

All About That Bounce is a locally operated family business. We rent bounce houses, wet and dry slides, combo units, obstacle courses and activities across Oldham, Shelby, Henry, Trimble and Jefferson counties, including La Grange and Shelbyville.

You talk to us, not a call center. Rena confirms every request personally, so your date isn’t booked until you hear back.

We deliver, set up, take down and pick up. Every inflatable is cleaned and sanitized after each use. We carry general liability insurance, and our inflatables are inspected each year through the Kentucky Department of Agriculture’s amusement ride safety program.

Paperwork, kept simpleEvery booking needs a signed rental agreement and general release.
Plan a happy day

Before the fun begins.

A few house rules keep every bounce safe. Questions? Just ask.

Setup space

Take the unit’s size, then add 5 feet to the width and 10 feet to the length. Leave room overhead too. Sizes vary, so check the one you’re booking.

Surface

Grass, pavement, concrete, hardwood or low carpet all work, indoors or out. Clear away rocks, sticks, sharp objects, mud, droppings and standing water. Please don’t mow for 3–4 days before delivery. Tell us your surface ahead of time so we bring the right anchoring.

Power

Each unit needs a dedicated 110V outlet on a 20-amp circuit within 75 feet. Some units need two. We bring the extension cords. A generator works too, but we don’t provide or rent them.

Weather

We can’t set up in active rain or high wind, and units can’t be used in rain or wind over 15 mph. Outdoor rentals are off when it’s 50°F or colder.

Weather cancellations are free up to 2 hours before drop-off. Keep the reservation and full payment applies, with no refund. We may cancel, postpone or pick up early if conditions turn unsafe. We call it rain when Weather.com’s hourly forecast shows a 50% or higher chance during party hours.

Supervision

An adult watches every unit at all times. Larger slides and obstacle courses need two adults. Confirm the count for your unit.

House rules

No food or drink, shoes, sharp objects, sand or silly string. No rough play, hanging on the netting or climbing the sidewalls. We leave a safety sheet at setup. Preventable damage brings a repair fee. Defects are never charged.

Cancellations

No deposit to hold your date. If plans change, let us know as soon as you can.
