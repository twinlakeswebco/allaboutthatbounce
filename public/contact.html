Our service area

We deliver across Oldham, Shelby, Henry, Trimble and Jefferson counties, including La Grange and Shelbyville.

Ways to reserve

Book online, call, text or email. We’ll talk through your event and help you pick the right unit.

Planning a commercial or public event? Pricing may differ, so give us a call to ask.

When to book

Peak season runs April through October. Book a few weeks ahead for a bounce house, and up to three months ahead for big events like school or church carnivals. Last-minute plans? Reach out anyway. We’re happy to help.
